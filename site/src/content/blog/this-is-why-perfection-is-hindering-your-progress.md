## It is not about being perfect, it is about being consistent

The "all or nothing" mentality is detrimental to weight loss for several reasons:

- It is not sustainable, rigid and extreme diets or exercise regimes are often not sustainable in the long-term, leading to burnout and eventually reverting back to old habits
- This mentality can lead to feelings of guilt and failure when setbacks occur, causing a negative impact on mental health and self-esteem
- Adopting an "all or nothing" approach often involves strict restriction of certain foods, which can lead to an unhealthy relationship with food and an increased likelihood of binge-eating
- This approach doesn't allow for a balanced lifestyle and can lead to physical and emotional exhaustion

Instead, it's important to adopt a healthy and sustainable approach to weight loss that involves making gradual changes to your diet and exercise habits, being mindful and accepting of slip-ups, and finding a balance that works for you and your lifestyle. Consistency is generally more important than being perfect. Here's why:

- Consistency allows you to set achievable goals and make progress over time, whereas the pursuit of perfection can be overwhelming and discouraging
- Consistency acknowledges that setbacks and mistakes will happen and allows for a more forgiving and realistic approach to progress
- Consistency is key to long-term success, as making small, sustainable changes over time is more effective than trying to achieve perfection quickly and then giving up
- Consistency promotes positive mental health by reducing stress and anxiety, and by allowing for a more forgiving and less rigid approach to progress
- Consistency and being OK with setbacks are more important than perfection, as it allows for achievable goals, realistic expectations, long-term success, and positive mental health.
